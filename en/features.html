Two learning methods

You decide how autonomously the AI shapes the learning process

Extend your existing Moodle courses with an AI learning companion – or let the tutor take over in guided learning mode.

Your Moodle course + AI tutor

Course-accompanying learning support

Your learners follow your Moodle course as usual. The AI tutor accompanies them along the way – answering questions, explaining content, creating exercises, providing fitting practical examples and much more. The course still sets the learning path.

AI-first mode

Guided learning mode

The AI tutor launches the Moodle course in full screen: it structures the learning path, sets learning goals, creates exercises and adapts the content to each learner's progress. Your Moodle course or a PDF serves as the knowledge base and can be shown in the side panel at any time.

Guided Learning Mode

The AI tutor works through the course with learners like a private tutor – step by step, in full screen with a progress display, adapted to their strengths and weaknesses.

  • It guides, asks questions, checks knowledge, remembers gaps, revisits and closes them – and only moves on once a chapter has truly sunk in.
  • A PDF upload in Moodle is enough: Alphalearn automatically detects the document's structure and learning objectives – no classic Moodle course required.
  • Every session can be ended at any time and resumed at the same point. Optionally, the tutor also searches the Moodle forums for answers.

Intelligent AI Chat

The AI tutor answers questions in real time based on your course content. It understands context, nuance and pedagogical goals.

Personalized Practice

The AI tutor creates personalized exercises based on course content and each learner's level – in different task formats. After each exercise, learners receive an evaluation and individual feedback with concrete improvement tips.

Active Learning Support

The AI tutor takes an active role in the learning process, offers proactive prompts and offers targeted help as soon as it is needed. All hints are contextual and match the currently opened course section.

Configurable Tutor Modes

In the Alphalearn Portal you define how the AI tutor behaves. Prebuilt templates represent proven didactic scenarios and can be adapted flexibly to your teaching and learning concept.

80+ Languages

The AI tutor speaks 80+ languages and automatically adapts to each learner's language and proficiency – support in their own language and at an explanation level that fits.

Long-Term Memory & Adaptation

The AI tutor recognizes individual strengths, weaknesses and progress and continuously adapts exercises, hints and explanations to the learner's level. Thanks to long-term memory, it also takes earlier interactions, learning patterns and recurring difficulties into account – beyond individual courses and, if needed, across several years.

Daily & Weekly Curriculum

Curriculum mode structures learning into daily and weekly units. A dedicated tab shows the plan, and a notification reminds learners of their daily unit when they log in.

Socratic Mode & Reflection

In Socratic mode, the AI tutor guides learners to their own understanding through targeted questions. Reflection questions prompt learners to think about what they have learned – what is clear, and where uncertainties remain.

Debate Mode

In debate mode, learners test arguments, switch perspectives and deepen their knowledge in dialogue with the AI tutor.

Research & Web Search

Learners explore topics independently and work through them in a structured way – the AI tutor supports research and web search.

Practical Examples

Concrete use cases and scenarios make abstract knowledge tangible and ease the transfer into real-world practice.

Verified Exercises

Our AI tutors generate exercises from your knowledge base and check results on their own – some education providers, however, prefer a human-in-the-loop approach. That is why you can upload your own exercises to the Alphalearn Portal, generate them with AI or create them manually. Send your verified exercises to entire Moodle courses or individual learners, who see them clearly in the chat, give them to your AI tutors as a teaching tool or move them directly into the knowledge base.

Intelligent Handover

Learners use the AI tutor not only for questions about course content, but also for study organization or technical problems. You decide whether another AI agent takes over or the question is forwarded to a real teacher. Complex or unresolved requests thus automatically reach the teacher or the support team.

Educator Dashboard

In the educator area of the Alphalearn Portal you see which learners need individual support, answer private messages and move useful responses into the knowledge base. A bell icon alerts you the moment learners need help. You refine your knowledge base based on analytics metrics or learner feedback.

No More Duplicate Data Maintenance

With Alphalearn Auto-Sync you only maintain your Moodle course — the AI tutor keeps itself up to date. It automatically reads all content of your Moodle course.

Course as Knowledge Base

An all-knowing AI tutor knows too much. With Moodle Course Sync, two clicks give you course-specific AI tutors that know exactly the right course knowledge: connect your Moodle platform to the Alphalearn Portal and use your existing Moodle courses as the knowledge base. It is built automatically in the background – no uploads, no technical setup.

Source Citations

Under each AI tutor response, the source used is shown – so it is traceable which document in the knowledge base the answer comes from. This builds trust; admins can toggle the display on or off.

All Moodle Activities

Not just pages and PDFs: Alphalearn synchronizes every activity in your Moodle course – quizzes, lessons, forums, calendar, videos, H5P, SCORM packages plus Articulate and iSpring courses. Forum discussions flow into the AI tutor's knowledge as well.

Direct Integrations

Besides Moodle, you connect your Alphalearn Portal directly to Microsoft SharePoint, Google Drive or Dropbox – for a seamless knowledge flow without manual uploads or duplicate data maintenance. All content remains in its source systems and is used as one unified data source.

Flexible Content Expansion

Expand the knowledge base in the Alphalearn Portal with websites, documents, notes, exercises or transcribed videos – all managed centrally.

Alphalytics Portal

The Alphalytics Portal – Alphalearn's AI analytics portal – evaluates interactions with the AI tutors and shows in a dashboard which questions learners ask, where comprehension problems arise and which content most often requires support. The Analytics Assistant singles out the most important metrics from all the data and delivers precise analyses and recommendations for action.

GDPR & Hosting in Germany

Hosted on trusted German servers with a GDPR-compliant architecture and the highest privacy standards. Your rights stay with you; a data processing agreement (DPA) is provided.

Learner Profile

In the Learner Profile, progress, strengths and weaknesses can be tracked per Moodle user and interpreted over time. It is also visible to learners themselves – so they see their successes and their development and stay motivated to improve.

Ethical Guardrails

AI needs clear boundaries. With the guardrails in the Alphalearn Portal you define what your AI tutors may talk about and whether rule violations are logged, redirected or blocked. Preconfigured guardrail templates based on UNESCO's ethical guidelines for AI in education can be activated directly in the portal, customized individually or extended with your own rules.

Agents & Usage Limits

In the Alphalearn Portal you structure your AI agents, assign them to courses and knowledge bases and set usage limits with precision. This keeps operation controllable, secure and scalable – even in professional learning environments.

Roles & Permissions

In the Alphalearn Portal you assign roles and permissions so that lecturers and trainers only see the courses and participants they are responsible for. Through role management you invite team members to collaborate on administration.

Alpha Flexible

The template for education providers with individual configuration needs: fine-tune every detail – from structured prompt modules and course-specific knowledge bases to optional curriculum context and clear usage limits – for an agent tailored exactly to your setup.
