Questions about pricing and plans

What the plans include and how limits are counted.

The free plan includes one AI tutor, 15,000 tokens per month, three data sources with up to 5 MB and one learner – yourself. It is meant for testing with your own content, requires no credit card and shows Alphabees branding.
We count the learners who are in your learning platform in a given month and have access to the AI tutor – whether or not they actually use it. Basic covers up to 100, Professional up to 250 and the API deal up to 500 learners.

A quick word on cookies

What do we need cookies for?

We only want to know whether this site works: which content gets read and where people drop off. Anonymous, no ads, never shared with third parties.

You can change this any time via “Cookie settings” in the footer.

What exactly do we measure?

With Google Analytics 4 we record pseudonymously:

  • Which pages are visited
  • Clicks on buttons, demo and sign-up
  • How far people scroll
  • Whether demo videos are started

No ad profiles, no selling of data, no merging with personal details. Details in our privacy policy and the cookie information.
