Admin roles
Managing access rights and roles in the Alphalearn Portal makes collaboration possible. As an admin, you can, for example, invite teachers to the Alphalearn Portal so they can create their own AI tutor, fill it with knowledge and integrate it into their e-learning courses.
Approval workflows
Certain tasks in the Alphalearn Portal must be approved first, and changes are logged. Helpful when many people work in the portal together.
Integration & Tools
Moodle Plugin
Dedicated Moodle plugin for Moodle desktop and the Moodle Mobile App for seamless integration into your Moodle learning environment.
ILIAS Plugin
Plugin for ILIAS: the AI tutor is embedded in your ILIAS courses and uses their course content as its knowledge base.
Course sync
Create an AI tutor template once and link it to your Moodle courses. Every tutor you place knows the content of its own course, plus any courses you link on purpose, e.g. modules 1 and 2 for module 3.
Moodle actions
The AI tutor points out dates from the Moodle calendar, knows the forum discussions in your course and takes course progress and grades into account. That way, it meets learners right where they are.
Alphalearn Portal
Central portal for creating and managing your AI tutors and knowledge bases, with access to Analytics, the Course Creator, user roles, an AI tutor usage overview and more.
Website integration
Embed the AI tutor as a chat widget on any website and use it as an AI chatbot (customer support, product advice, etc.).
Integration into other LMSs
Integrating the AI tutor directly into the SCORM, xAPI or HTML file of your e-learning lets you use it in almost any learning management system (LMS).
LTI 1.3 interface
With the LTI 1.3 interface, you can embed AI tutors in other learning platforms in a standards-compliant way, with secure single sign-on and course and user context passed along, no separate plugin required.
Cloud services
Connect Google Drive, Microsoft SharePoint or Dropbox to the Alphalearn Portal. Your content stays in its original systems, and the knowledge base automatically stays in sync with the source.
MCP connectors
With MCP connectors (Model Context Protocol), you connect your AI agents to other systems and data sources so they can retrieve content and take actions there.
Didactic configuration
In the Alphalearn Portal, each tutor can be assigned a template that controls its behavior. Every template can be customized in detail.
Agent skills
Extend your AI agents with additional abilities, called skills, and define which tasks each agent takes on.
Practice mode
The AI generates interactive quiz questions on the learning content you choose. Once the learner has answered, the AI evaluates and corrects the answers and gives individual feedback and encouragement.
Verified exercises
Create exercises in the Alphalearn Portal with AI from your knowledge base, manually or by upload. Release the exercises you have reviewed for practice mode, or send them to learners or entire Moodle courses.
-
Dialogue modes
Different interaction modes such as Socratic dialogue (answers with follow-up questions), declarative dialogue (a didactic frame around every answer) or debate mode (testing arguments, switching perspectives) and more.
Homework protection
The AI tutor doesn’t hand out finished solutions to homework or exam tasks. Instead, it guides learners to their own solution with hints and follow-up questions.
Active learning support
Proactive help via pop-up messages for the chapter that is currently open. The AI tutor actively offers its help and delivers topic-relevant support right in the course.
Adaptive learner profiles
Optional: the AI tutor recognizes strengths, weaknesses and progress and adapts exercises and explanations accordingly. Thanks to long-term memory, it takes earlier interactions into account. Learners can view their profile.
Individual study plan
Study plan mode splits learning into daily and weekly units. A dedicated tab shows the plan, and a notification reminds learners of the day’s unit when they log in.
Source references
Answers include references to the documents used from the knowledge base.
Chat history
The AI tutor keeps the conversation within a learning session, so it can pick up the last topic even after the chat window has been closed.
Analytics
Analysis of usage and learning progress + an AI data analyst that analyzes and interprets the analytics data. Export usage reports, get recommendations for action and more.
Quality control
Learners can rate every AI answer with a thumbs up or down. All ratings come together in the Alphalearn Portal, so you can spot weak answers and fill specific gaps in the knowledge base.
Course Creator
Describe the course you need in the chat: the AI turns it into a complete Moodle course with course name, learning objectives, sections, assignments and tests. You can adjust everything in the editor.
-
Teacher features
Teacher features with the Alpha Twin template: teachers create an AI agent in their own style, see where learners need support, view learner profiles, send assignments or exams, manage FAQs and turn helpful answers into knowledge suggestions. Complex questions are forwarded to the teacher.
-
Smart handover
For questions about study organization or technical problems, you decide whether another AI agent takes over or the question is forwarded to a teacher.
-
Guided learning mode
The AI tutor works through a Moodle course or a PDF step by step with learners in full screen, checks their knowledge and only moves on once a chapter has sunk in.
Didactic parsing
Alphalearn reads your Moodle course or the PDFs uploaded there from a didactic perspective and recognizes the course structure, learning objectives and exercises. This is the foundation for Guided learning mode.

A quick word on cookies

What do we need cookies for?

We only want to know whether this site works: which content gets read and where people drop off. Anonymous, no ads, never shared with third parties.

You can change this any time via “Cookie settings” in the footer.

What exactly do we measure?

With Google Analytics 4 we record pseudonymously:

  • Which pages are visited
  • Clicks on buttons, demo and sign-up
  • How far people scroll
  • Whether demo videos are started

No ad profiles, no selling of data, no merging with personal details. Details in our privacy policy and the cookie information.
